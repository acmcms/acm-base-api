# MAGIC.md — acm-base-api

Team-owned. Durable findings and gotchas for this repo, not investigation narration.

## For keeper-acm / keeper-ae3

- **`SkinnerAbstract` is the second handler of the `/!/skin/` URL space, and it answers for an abstract skin.** `SkinnerAbstract.onQuery` (`java/ru/myx/ae3/skinner/SkinnerAbstract.java:642`) matches `!/` at `:656` and `skin` at `:667`, takes the name at `:672`, and dispatches through `Context.getServer(...).getSkinner(name)` at `:673`. A name the server does not hold answers `Bad skin chooser request, not found` there, at `:674-679`. `ae3.sdk`'s `TargetInterfaceAbstract.onQuery` (`java/ru/myx/ae3/i3/TargetInterfaceAbstract.java:74`) does the same job through `SkinScanner.getSystemSkinner` at `:92`, and its own `FIXME` at `:94` names the duplication. So the javadoc at `TargetInterfaceAbstract.java:21`, "the one place the `/!/` URL space is defined", is not true of the skin branch. Which of the two a host-level request reaches is settled by the dispatch chain, and no class states it.
- **`<abstract>` is a listing filter, not an access gate.** `SkinnerAbstract.isAbstract` (`:627`) reads the `abstract` key from `skin.settings.xml` with `false` as its absent value. Measured on 2026-11-09 over 13,974 source files under `/Volumes/workspace/myx`, with `incoming/`, `.git/`, `bin/`, `build/` and `tmp/` pruned: two calling sites, being `acm-srv-acmcms/java/ru/myx/cm5/control/sharing/LookupSkinner.java:41`, which filters a share's skin list, and `skin-choose-session-interface/choose.htm:162`, which skips abstract skins in the interface chooser. That `choose.htm` sits in three byte-identical copies, `acm-srv-acmcms/resources/skin`, `acm-cvs/sys-current` and `acm-cvs/sys-release`, all at md5 `8fbbdffbdf2406df9bce88d48ba8b4d4`. Neither `onQuery` above consults it, so an abstract skin still answers a direct `/!/skin/<name>/` request. `vlapan-default` declares `true` at its `skin.settings.xml:8`, and a live run recorded in `unit-test/MAGIC.md:56` got 200 for `/!/skin/vlapan-default/showargs.htm` against 404 for the same name under `skin-standard-html`. Its own folder holds `showargs.htm.ecma` and no `showargs.htm`, so that 200 came from the renderer and not from a static passthrough.
- **The prototype handover has two doors, and they take different calls. Do not cite one for the other.** The reply side is the failover inside `handleReply`, at `:586-609`: it runs `handleReplyMapOnce` at `:588` and, on a miss, calls `getSkinner(this.prototypeSkin)` at `:595` and `skinner.handleReplyOnce(response)` at `:598`. The query side is `renderQueryImpl` at `:772-786`: it calls `skinner.onQuery(query)` at `:776`, and `:760-763` declines first for an identifier of one character or less. So a bare `/` never reaches the prototype, while a template name that misses does. Read from source on 2026-11-09 against the `vlapan` cascade; no render was run.
